About Our Bible College

Our Bible College is dedicated to equipping believers with sound biblical doctrine, leadership principles, and practical ministry training. We prepare students to serve with excellence in the church, community, and beyond.

Academic Instruction

CFCI Bible College students meet the highest standards of excellence; they develop their intellect and increase their knowledge of God. Students become lifelong learners across a wide array of disciplines, strengthening their understanding of their Christ-centred calling in an engaging classroom setting.

Ministry Leadership Training

CFCI Bible College equips students to be the hands and feet of Jesus through elite hands-on ministry training and empowers students to take ownership in every area of church life. Students receive leadership training and opportunities to develop the skills needed to change the world.

Spiritual & Character Formation

CFCI Bible College challenges students to live a life of excellence through living a healthy, active lifestyle and growing their leadership abilities. While at CFCI Bible College, students develop Christ-like character through time spent in relationships with mentors and peers.

Scholarship

CFC Greater Works Campus offers scholarships to eligible students. Review the guidelines below and complete the application process.

Scholarship Guidelines

  • Spiritual Growth and Commitment: Genuine love for God and a desire to serve in ministry.
  • Age Requirement: Applicants must be between 18 and 30 years old.
  • Academic Performance: Maintain a 70%+ average grade per quarter.
  • Attendance: 100% attendance in classes, practicals, and church requirements.
  • Awards: Determined by the SBI Scholarship Review Committee. Awards may increase up to full tuition and may cover up to two years of continuous full-time enrolment.
  • Conditions: Scholarships are non-transferable, must be used within the award year, and may be suspended or revoked if the student is not in good standing or violates the honor code.
